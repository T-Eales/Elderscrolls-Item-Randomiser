Welcome to Tamriel Forge

This tool rolls up randomised Elder Scrolls-style items for tabletop & MMO worldbuilding, complete with full stats and cost.

  • ⚔ Weapons — roll a Quality, Material, Item Type, and Enchantment, with damage, penetration, range, encumbrance and cost worked out automatically.
  • 🎒 Items — roll a Quality and an Item Type (clothing, tools, arcane items, livestock, transportation) with encumbrance and cost worked out automatically.
  • 📜 Spell Scrolls — roll a Level and a Spell, with magicka cost, XP to learn, and effect (damage etc.) worked out for that level.

Pick a tab above to get started. Inside each tab, open a panel and tick only the specific options you want — leave a panel closed and untouched to allow anything in it — then click Randomise.
